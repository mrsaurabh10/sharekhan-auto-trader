The 9/21 crossover

Closed spot bars. Three option modes. Every decision traceable.

Research replay only. Inputs are unverified until reconciled with the broker. Missing premiums and fees stay unavailable. ATM selling has no margin or capital feasibility model.

Rules and data contract

Signals use 9 and 21 EMA, separately SMA-seeded, on complete 5m/15m/30m bars aligned to 09:15. History carries between complete sessions. Equal EMAs retain the last nonzero ordering. Initial ordering is not a crossover. Fill at the exact next minute open after bar close; window endpoints are inclusive. Opposite crosses always exit; reverse only inside the entry window. 15:00 square-off takes priority. No stop-loss or target is specified.

Spot CSV: timestamp,open,high,low,close. Option CSV: timestamp,contractId,listedOn,expiry,strike,right,lotSize,open,high,low,close. ISO timestamps have no offset and mean IST minute starts. NIFTY / NSE_FNO only; CE or PE. One lot per trade uses contract metadata. ATM rounds spot signal close to nearest 50, ties up. ITM5 is 250 points deeper. Bullish ATM sell shorts PE; bearish shorts CE. The same contract is held to exit. All 375 minute bars are required per observed spot session.

Optional fee JSON: array of dated schedules with from,to,source,brokeragePerOrder,sttSell,exchange,sebi,stampBuy,gst,ipft. Rates are fractions, e.g. 0.18 for 18% GST. No current fee schedule is silently applied to history. GST applies to brokerage, exchange, SEBI and IPFT. Contract-note rounding and additional broker charges can differ. Gross-only reports omit net metrics.
